# Harborline architectural materials

- Factory Brick by Rob Tuytel. [Poly Haven source](https://polyhaven.com/a/factory_brick)
- Worn Corrugated Iron by Jenelle van Heerden, Dimitrios Savva. [Poly Haven source](https://polyhaven.com/a/worn_corrugated_iron)
- Blue Plaster Weathered by Amal Kumar. [Poly Haven source](https://polyhaven.com/a/blue_plaster_weathered)
- Grey Plaster by Rob Tuytel. [Poly Haven source](https://polyhaven.com/a/grey_plaster)

These photogrammetry color maps are provided under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The original 1K JPG downloads were verified against their published MD5 checksums and converted to WebP for the game.

Building geometry, street signs, graphics, glazing and fixtures were created for Harborline.
