Converted from FBX to glTF. Textures were compressed for browser use and root motion was removed from the locomotion clips.

Living City uses independently animated instances of this resident. The wardrobe shader, three headwear models, jump and contact poses were authored for HARBORLINE; wardrobe variants share the original character geometry, textures and locomotion clips.

Elena Reed uses Female Adult 05 with the matching female idle, walk and run clips.
Jack Bennett uses Male Adult 04 with the shared male locomotion clips.
Both additions come from Microsoft Rocketbox revision 0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9 under the same MIT license. The glTF conversions use embedded reduced-resolution textures and in-place locomotion.
