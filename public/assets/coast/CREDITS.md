# Meridian Coast

- Sand 02 by Charlotte Baglioni. [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_02)
- Rock Boulder Cracked by Dario Barresi and Dimitrios Savva. [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_cracked)

Both materials use [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The 1K source color, OpenGL normal and Sand 02 roughness JPEGs were verified against the Poly Haven API checksums, resized to 512px and converted to WebP. The coast, seabed, rocks, vegetation, pier, boat geometry and procedural effects were authored for Harborline.
