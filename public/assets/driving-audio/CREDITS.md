# Driving audio credits

- `ignition.wav` adapts `generic_car_startengine.wav` from [Car engine start 01 by looneybits](https://opengameart.org/content/car-engine-start-01), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Converted to mono 24 kHz and reduced gain by 3 dB.
- `rpm-low.wav` and `rpm-high.wav` adapt `loop_0.wav` and `loop_5_0.wav` from [Racing car engine sound loops by domasx2](https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Converted to mono 24 kHz.
- `skid.wav` adapts `skid-loop.wav`, which plays during lateral tire slip. Converted to mono 24 kHz and reduced gain by 3 dB. [Car Tire Skid Squealing by Mike Koenig (SoundBible), submitted by qubodup](https://opengameart.org/content/car-tire-skid-squealing) · [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- `brake.wav` uses the opening 0.64 seconds of a tire squeal for firm braking. Filtered, faded, reduced in gain by 6 dB, and converted to mono 24 kHz PCM. [Screeching Tires #1 by Dorian Clair on BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com/screeching-tires-1-s2368.html) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
