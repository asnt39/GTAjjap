# Driving audio credits

- `ignition.wav` adapts `generic_car_startengine.wav` from [Car engine start 01 by looneybits](https://opengameart.org/content/car-engine-start-01), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Converted to mono 24 kHz and reduced gain by 3 dB.
- `rpm-low.wav` and `rpm-high.wav` adapt `loop_0.wav` and `loop_5_0.wav` from [Racing car engine sound loops by domasx2](https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Converted to mono 24 kHz.
- `skid.wav` adapts `skid-loop.wav` from [Car Tire Skid Squealing by Mike Koenig (SoundBible), submitted by qubodup](https://opengameart.org/content/car-tire-skid-squealing), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Converted to mono 24 kHz and reduced gain by 3 dB. The game blends it in response to slip and sustained braking.
