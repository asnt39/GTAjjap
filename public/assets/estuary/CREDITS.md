# Estuary landscape

Grass Path 3 by Rob Tuytel, [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_path_3), [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness JPEGs were verified against the Poly Haven file API MD5 values, resized to 512 × 512 and encoded as WebP. The three maps are used on the outer embankments. The landscaping, buildings, signs and moving doors are original Harborline geometry. The park trees also reuse the licensed [street foliage](../street/CREDITS.md).

The garden lawn uses [Grass Ground by Charlotte Baglioni](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_ground), CC0 1.0. Its 1K diffuse map was checked against the published MD5 and resized to a 512 px WebP.

Garden trunks use a Blender-decimated derivative of Poly Haven's [Tree Small 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02), CC0 1.0. Five instances share one 19 kB GLB near Foundry Garden. Branch silhouettes take over at distance. The normal and roughness detail from Grass Path 3 is used on the lawn, with a single colour map on the distant embankments.
