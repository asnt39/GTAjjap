# Field sound credits

- `step-boots-{1,2,3}.wav`, `step-wood-{1,2,3}.wav`, and `step-grass-{1,2,3}.wav` use samples from [Footsteps on different surfaces](https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-on-different-surfaces), compiled by congusbongus. The source recordings are by swuing, [concrete](https://freesound.org/people/swuing/sounds/38873/), [wood](https://freesound.org/people/swuing/sounds/38876/), and [grass](https://freesound.org/people/swuing/sounds/38874/). [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Converted to mono 24 kHz WAV.
- `step-metal-{1,2,3}.wav` uses samples from the same collection, derived from [boots on aluminum ladder 01 by Eelke](https://freesound.org/people/Eelke/sounds/462598/). [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Converted to mono 24 kHz WAV.
- `step-gravel-{1,2,3}.wav` uses samples from the same collection, derived from [Gravel Footsteps by Ali_6868](https://freesound.org/people/Ali_6868/packs/21608/). [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Converted to mono 24 kHz WAV.
- `collision.wav` is converted to mono 24 kHz WAV and reduced to 65% amplitude from [Crash Collision by qubodup](https://opengameart.org/content/crash-collision). [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- `blast.wav` is converted to mono 24 kHz WAV and reduced to 60% amplitude from [Mechanical Explosion by Spring Spring](https://opengameart.org/content/mechanical-explosion). [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
