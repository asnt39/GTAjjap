# Field sound credits

- `wood01.wav`, `wood02.wav`, `wood03.wav`, `stone01.wav`, `mud02.wav`, `leaves01.wav`, `leaves02.wav`, and `gravel.wav` are converted to mono 24 kHz WAV from the matching OGG files in [Different Steps on Wood, Stone, Leaves, Gravel and Mud](https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud), by TinyWorlds.
