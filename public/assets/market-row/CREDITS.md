# Market Row and dockside details

The models below are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). HARBORLINE resized their PBR maps to 384 pixels and embedded them in GLB files. The bin and manhole also use simplified meshes. The rooms, shutters' placement, signage, stairs and environmental layout were authored for HARBORLINE.
