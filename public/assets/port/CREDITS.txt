Harborline port environment

Poly Haven, Concrete Floor 02
https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02
CC0 1.0. The 1K base-color and OpenGL normal scans were converted to WebP for the concrete quay.

Poly Haven, Rusty Metal 02
https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_02
CC0 1.0. The 1K base-color scan supplies weathering for the crane paint and cargo container atlas.

The terminal, vessels, cranes, quay furniture, signs, cargo liveries and rib patterns were modeled or drawn for Harborline. The water shader is original.

Three pieces of playable scenery use Poly Haven's downloadable CC0 1.0 models.
The GLBs embed base colour, tangent-space normal and roughness/metalness maps
at 384, 256 and 128 pixels. Repeated copies use instancing. Original author
credits and release terms are available at https://polyhaven.com/license.

quay-fender.glb — Old Tyre by MP
https://polyhaven.com/a/old_tyre
The tyre profile replaces the rectangular fenders on the near quay wall.
Decimated from 2,880 to 518 triangles. One material for 14 fenders.

archive-bench.glb — Painted Wooden Bench by Kirill Sannikov
https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_wooden_bench
Two panelled 630-triangle seats occupy the old chart archive bench footprint.

chandlery-crate.glb — Wooden Crate 02 by James Ray Cock and Jurita Burger
https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_crate_02
The open fitted timber box replaces the six solid parcels at the chandlery.
Lid omitted; reduced from 4,492 to 1,078 triangles per open crate.
Loaded and released with the Tide Market district.
